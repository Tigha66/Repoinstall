# Demo Video Script

**Length:** 45–60 seconds

---

## Scene 1: Customer sends message (0–5 seconds)

**Screen:** WhatsApp chat on phone

**Customer message:**
> "Hi, do you do end-of-tenancy cleaning?"

*(Message appears as if just sent)*

---

## Scene 2: Assistant replies instantly (5–10 seconds)

**Screen:** Same WhatsApp chat

**Assistant reply:**
> "Hi, yes — we can help with end-of-tenancy cleaning in London. To prepare a quote, can I ask a few quick questions? First, what postcode is the property in?"

*(Reply appears instantly — show the speed)*

---

## Scene 3: Customer replies with postcode (10–13 seconds)

**Screen:** Same WhatsApp chat

**Customer:**
> "SW6"

---

## Scene 4: Assistant asks property size (13–16 seconds)

**Assistant:**
> "Great, thanks. What type of property is it?"

**Customer:**
> "2-bed flat"

---

## Scene 5: Assistant asks extras (16–20 seconds)

**Assistant:**
> "Do you need any extras? Common extras include oven, fridge, carpets, windows."

**Customer:**
> "Oven and windows"

---

## Scene 6: Assistant asks date (20–25 seconds)

**Assistant:**
> "What date would you ideally like the cleaning done?"

**Customer:**
> "Friday morning"

---

## Scene 7: Assistant asks for name and number (25–30 seconds)

**Assistant:**
> "What's your name and best number for the team to confirm the quote?"

**Customer:**
> "Sarah, 07700 900123"

---

## Scene 8: Assistant confirms (30–35 seconds)

**Assistant:**
> "Thanks Sarah. I've passed this to the SwiftClean London team. They'll review the details and contact you shortly with availability and a quote."

---

## Scene 9: Owner receives lead summary (35–50 seconds)

**Screen:** Email or Google Sheet on laptop/desktop

**Show the lead summary:**

```
New cleaning lead captured
Business: SwiftClean London
Customer name: Sarah
Customer phone: 07700 900123
Service: End-of-tenancy cleaning
Postcode: SW6
Property type: 2-bed flat
Furnished status: Not specified
Extras: Oven + windows
Preferred date: Friday morning
Urgency: Not specified
Lead quality: High
Suggested next step: Send quote or call customer
```

---

## Scene 10: Closing text overlay (50–60 seconds)

**Screen:** Clean background with text overlay

**Text:**
> "This is what your business receives instead of a messy WhatsApp chat — a quote-ready lead summary."

**Subtext:**
> "7-day free trial for London cleaning companies."

**URL:**
> callpilotvoice.co.uk

---

## Production Notes

- Use a real WhatsApp chat mockup (not a real customer's phone)
- Keep the assistant messages short and natural
- Show the speed of replies — this is a key selling point
- The lead summary should be the hero moment — make it clean and readable
- End with a clear CTA: "Start your free trial"
- Background music: light, professional, unobtrusive
- Total length: 45–60 seconds
