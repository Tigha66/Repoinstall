# Sales Materials

## Outreach Message (WhatsApp / Instagram DM)

```
Hi, I saw that your cleaning business takes quote enquiries online and through WhatsApp.

I'm offering a 7-day free trial of a WhatsApp receptionist for London cleaning companies.

It replies instantly, asks customers the right quote questions, collects details like postcode, property size, service type, extras and preferred date, then sends you a clean lead summary.

I can build a free demo using your actual services so you can test whether it saves time and captures more enquiries.

Would you like me to show you a quick example for your cleaning business?
```

---

## Sales Call Script

### Question 1:
**"Do most of your cleaning enquiries come through WhatsApp?"**

Listen for: How they currently handle enquiries, volume, pain points.

### Question 2:
**"Do you ever miss messages while working, driving, cleaning, or after hours?"**

Listen for: Missed leads, delayed replies, lost customers.

### Question 3:
**"What are the same questions customers keep asking?"**

Listen for: Repetitive questions, time spent on qualification.

### Question 4:
**"What details do you need before giving a cleaning quote?"**

Listen for: Postcode, property size, extras, date — confirm what the assistant will collect.

### Question 5:
**"If those details came to you already organised, would that save you time?"**

Listen for: Confirmation of value.

### Pitch:
**"That's exactly what I set up. Your WhatsApp receptionist replies instantly, asks the customer the right quote questions, and sends you a clean summary. You still control the conversation, but you don't have to manually answer every first message yourself."**

### Close:
**"I'm offering a 7-day free trial. If it helps you capture better enquiries, the founding client price is £149 setup plus £49/month for the first 2 months."**

---

## Objection Handling

**"I don't need AI / automation."**
> "It's not about replacing anything — it's about making sure you never miss a customer who messages you on WhatsApp. The assistant just collects the details so you can focus on quoting and booking."

**"How much does it cost?"**
> "The founding client offer is £149 setup plus £49/month for the first 2 months. After that it's £99/month for the Starter plan. But let's start with the 7-day free trial so you can see if it works for your business first."

**"Is it spam? Will it message my customers?"**
> "No. It only replies to customers who message you first. No outbound messages, no marketing, no spam. It's purely a receptionist — it answers when someone gets in touch."

**"What if it gives the wrong information?"**
> "It only collects details — it doesn't give prices or guarantees. It passes the information to you, and you stay in control of the quote. If anything looks off, you can step in at any time."

**"I'm not technical."**
> "You don't need to be. I set everything up for you. You just need to test it and tell me if anything needs changing."

**"I'll think about it."**
> "Of course. The 7-day free trial is completely free — no card, no commitment. You can test it with real enquiries and see if it saves you time. If it doesn't help, you walk away. What have you got to lose?"
