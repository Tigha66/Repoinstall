Carte — Outreach Emails

10 personalised emails · click-ready · links stay clickable when pasted into Gmail.
📌 How to use: click Copy (rich) → open Gmail → Compose → paste in the body. The demo & full-site links stay clickable. Put the address from “To” in the recipient field and the Subject line in. Or click Open in Gmail to auto-start a draft (then paste the rich body over the plain one to get clickable links).
