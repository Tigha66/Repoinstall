Questions

Good to know

Is the concept really free?

Yes. I rebuild your homepage as a working concept before you pay anything. If you don't like it, you keep it and we part as friends.

How long does it take?

Once you approve the concept and send your photos and details, the full site is usually live within about a week.

Will it keep my booking system?

Absolutely. I integrate your existing system — OpenTable, Resy, Tock, SevenRooms or Toast — nothing changes for your guests except a faster, nicer experience.

Do I need to provide photos?

Your own photos make it shine, and I'll guide you. If you need new ones, I can advise on a shoot. Concepts use stock to show the look.

Can I update it myself?

The care plan covers updates so you never have to — just message me. Prefer to self-manage? That's fine too.

Do you work outside the UK?

Yes — I build for restaurants and cafés anywhere. Everything is handled remotely.
