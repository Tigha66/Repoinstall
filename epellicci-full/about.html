Our Story

Bethnal Green · Since 1900

1900

The same corner,
four generations

Priamo Pellicci opened the café in 1900. The marquetry frontage and Art Deco interior, added in 1946, are now Grade II listed — and the family still runs it today.

Generations of East Enders have grown up over its tables. The welcome hasn't changed in over a century.
