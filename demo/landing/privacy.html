Who we are

RingBack provides a "missed-call text-back" service for local businesses: when a customer calls a business that uses our service and the call is not answered, the caller receives a single automated message offering assistance.

SMS / Messaging Terms

How consent (opt-in) works. The consumer initiates contact by calling the business's published phone number (shown on the business's website, listings, advertising, business cards, or vehicle signage). If the call is not answered, the consumer receives one automated reply message in response to that inbound call. The inbound call is the opt-in. We do not use purchased, rented, or third-party marketing lists.

Sample message:
"Thanks for calling [Business]! Sorry we missed your call — how can we help? Reply STOP to opt out."
  • Message frequency: low — typically a single reply per missed call; conversational thereafter only if the consumer replies.
  • Opt-out: reply STOP at any time to stop messages.
  • Help: reply HELP for assistance, or email hello@callpilotvoice.co.uk.
  • Message & data rates may apply, charged by the consumer's carrier.

Data we collect & how we use it

We process the caller's phone number and message content solely to deliver the text-back reply on behalf of the business the consumer called. We use it only to provide this service.

We do not sell or share SMS opt-in/consent data, or phone numbers collected for messaging, with any third parties for their own marketing purposes. Data is shared only with the messaging carrier/provider strictly to deliver the message.

Your rights

You can opt out at any time (reply STOP), and you can request deletion of your data by emailing hello@callpilotvoice.co.uk. We comply with applicable UK GDPR/PECR and US messaging regulations.
