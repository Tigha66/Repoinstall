Service

RingBack provides automated SMS/WhatsApp messaging for small businesses: a courtesy reply to missed calls, appointment booking confirmations, and reminders. Messages are sent on behalf of the business to people who have contacted that business.

Messaging & consent

Recipients receive messages because they called or contacted the business (they initiate contact). Message frequency varies by interaction. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of further messages at any time, or HELP for assistance.

Opt-out

Replying STOP unsubscribes you immediately; you will receive no further messages. Reply START to resubscribe. For help, reply HELP or email hello@callpilotvoice.co.uk.

Privacy

We process phone numbers only to deliver these service messages and do not sell personal data. See our Privacy Policy.
