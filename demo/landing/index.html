Questions

Do I need a new phone or number?

No. We use your existing business number and your WhatsApp. Nothing changes for your team.

Is it annoying or spammy for customers?

The opposite — they only get a message because they just called you and you couldn't answer. It's a helpful courtesy reply they're expecting, not marketing.

How fast can I be live?

Usually within 24 hours of signing up for the free trial. We handle the entire setup.

What happens after the 7-day trial?

If it's recovered customers for you (it usually does within days), you pick a plan. If not, you owe nothing — no card is taken to start.

What if the caller doesn't use WhatsApp?

On the Pro plan we automatically fall back to a standard SMS so no caller is missed.
