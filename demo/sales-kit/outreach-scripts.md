# Outreach Scripts — copy, tweak the [brackets], send

> Goal of every message: get them to **watch the 30–90s demo** and say yes to a
> **free 2-week trial**. Nothing else. No price, no features dump.

---

## 1. In person (highest conversion — do ~8 of your 20)
Walk in during a quiet hour (mid-morning / mid-afternoon, never lunch).

> "Hi — are you the owner/manager? I'm [name], I'm local. Quick one: I built a
> tool that automatically sends a WhatsApp to customers who call when you're busy
> or can't pick up — so you stop losing them to voicemail. Can I show you 30
> seconds on my phone?"

*(show the live demo — call the number, let the WhatsApp land)*

> "That just happened with zero effort from you. I'm setting it up free for a
> couple of [area] businesses for two weeks — no card, no commitment. Want me to
> switch it on for you this week?"

**If yes:** get their WhatsApp number + a good time to set up. That's it.

---

>

>
> 90-second demo: [Loom link]
>
> I'm giving a few [area] businesses a **free 2-week trial** — no card, I set it
> all up. If it recovers even a couple of customers, you'll see it instantly.
>
> Worth a quick go? Reply "yes" and I'll get it running this week.
>
> [name] · [phone] · [area]

## 3. WhatsApp / website chat / Instagram DM (~6 of your 20)
Many already have a "Chat on WhatsApp" button — use their own channel.

> Hi! Quick one for the owner 👋 I built a tool that auto-sends a WhatsApp to
> customers who call [Business] when you're busy (missed-call text-back), so you
> stop losing them. 90-sec demo: [Loom]. Offering a free 2-week trial to a few
> [area] businesses — want one set up this week?

---

## 4. Follow-ups (the money is in these — most replies come on #2)
**Follow-up A (2–3 days later):**
> Hi [name] — just floating this back up. Happy to set up the free trial so you
> can see it work on a couple of real calls, no commitment. Want me to switch it on?

**Follow-up B (4–5 days after A, then stop):**
> Last nudge 🙂 — I'll be setting these up for [area] businesses this week. If
> you'd like [Business] included on the free trial, just say the word.

---

## 5. Objection handling
- **"How much is it?"** → "Free for the 2-week trial. After that it's [£49–99]/mo
  if you want to keep it — but let's first see what it recovers for you."
- **"We don't miss many calls."** → "Perfect — then it costs you nothing and
  quietly catches the few you do. Those are the ones worth the most. Free to try?"
- **"Is this allowed / spammy?"** → "It only replies to people who *just called
  you* — they're expecting contact. It's a courtesy text-back, not marketing."
- **"I'm too busy to set it up."** → "You don't do anything — I set it all up and
  manage it. You just tell me your WhatsApp number."
- **"Send me info."** → Send the Loom + `pricing-one-pager.md`, then follow up in 2 days.

## 7. Professional variant — "WhatsApp receptionist" (any niche)
Use this when you're NOT leaning on the cleaning-peer angle (e.g. barbers, dentists,
trades, or if the peer line doesn't feel right). Matches the landing page positioning.

**Subject (email):** `[Business] — your missed calls, answered automatically`

**Message (WhatsApp / DM / email):**
> Hi [name] 👋 Quick one — when a customer calls [Business] and you can't pick up,
> that enquiry is usually lost. I set up a **WhatsApp receptionist** that instantly
> texts those missed callers back so they start a conversation with you instead of
> a competitor. 30-sec demo + how it works: **https://get.callpilotvoice.co.uk**
> Happy to set it up **free for 7 days** for you — want me to? — [you], hello@callpilotvoice.co.uk

**One-liner for in-person / phone:**
> "I set businesses up with a WhatsApp receptionist — when you miss a call, the
> customer automatically gets a WhatsApp so you don't lose them. Free for 7 days,
> I set it all up. Want to try it?"

**When to use which opener:**
- **Cleaning businesses** → the peer version (§1–3): *"I run a cleaning company too…"* (warm, high trust).
- **Everyone else** → this professional version. Same demo, same trial, same price.

---

## ⚖️ Keep it compliant (UK)
- Outreach to businesses: email/LinkedIn/their-own-chat is fine; always give an
  easy opt-out and don't mass-blast unsolicited WhatsApp.
- The *product* only messages people who **just called the client** = expected,
  service-level contact. Keep trial messages to that — no marketing blasts.
