These SMS / Messaging Terms apply to text messages sent by CallPilot Voice.

Program summary. By opting in, you agree to receive SMS messages from CallPilot Voice relating to appointment confirmations, reminders, and service notifications. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help.

How you opt in

Consent is obtained in one of the following ways:

  • Verbal opt-in during a phone call with our AI receptionist or staff, captured and logged with your phone number, date, and time. The verbal disclosure includes the program description, message frequency, "message and data rates may apply", and STOP/HELP instructions.
  • Written opt-in where you provide your number and agree to receive messages.

Sample message

"Hi, this is CallPilot. Your appointment is confirmed for Tuesday at 3:00 PM. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help."

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. You will receive one confirmation message and no further messages unless you opt in again.
