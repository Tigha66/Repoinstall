This Privacy Policy explains how CallPilot Voice ("CallPilot", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our AI phone receptionist and related communications, including SMS text messages.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details you or our customers provide, such as your name and mobile phone number.
  • Call & message data — records of calls handled by our AI receptionist, message content, timestamps, and delivery status.
  • Consent records — evidence of your opt-in to receive messages, including the date, time, and method of consent.
  • Technical data — limited logs needed to operate and secure the service.

2. How we use information

  • To answer calls, take messages, book appointments, and send related confirmations and reminders.
  • To send SMS messages you have opted in to receive.
  • To provide customer support and maintain the security and reliability of the service.
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations.
Mobile information & SMS consent — no sharing. No mobile information (including phone numbers and SMS opt-in/consent data) will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing with subcontractors strictly to support our services (for example, message delivery providers) is permitted, but text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third parties for their own purposes.

3. SMS / text messaging

We only send SMS to individuals who have provided prior express consent (opt-in). You can opt out at any time by replying STOP, or get help by replying HELP. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. See our SMS / Messaging Terms for full details.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers who help us operate (e.g. telecommunications and message-delivery partners) under contracts that protect your data, or where required by law. As stated above, SMS opt-in and consent data are not shared with third parties for their own use.

5. Data retention & security

We retain information only as long as necessary for the purposes described or as required by law, and we apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it.

6. Your rights

Subject to applicable law (including UK GDPR), you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and you may withdraw consent at any time. Contact us at hello@callpilotvoice.co.uk.
