Our Story

Refined Southern · Austin

The Kitchen

Southern roots,
modern craft

Olamaie celebrates the ingredients and traditions of the American South — heirloom grains from Carolina, Gulf seafood, and vegetables from Texas farms — through a refined, seasonal prix fixe.

Set in a restored downtown house, the room is warm and unhurried; the cooking is precise, generous, and rooted in place. Michelin-recognized for its craft.
