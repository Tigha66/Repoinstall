Our Story

قصتنا · The magic of Arab hospitality

Curated by Tashkeel

A celebration of Emirati culture

Gerbou — "welcome to our humble abode" — is a contemporary fine-dining restaurant that celebrates Emirati hospitality and heritage. Curated by Tashkeel, it sets centuries-old flavours within a refined, modern setting.

Every plate honours the generosity at the heart of Arab culture: an invitation, warmly extended.

The Kitchen

Tradition, modern technique

Head Chef Ninad Naresh Salvi brings depth honed in Michelin-starred kitchens, working alongside Sous Chef Raed Arabi to reinterpret Emirati classics with precision and respect.

Heritage grains, dried lime, slow fire — reimagined for today's table.
