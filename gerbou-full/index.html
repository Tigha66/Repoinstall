Welcome to our humble abode

Gerbou is a contemporary fine-dining restaurant that celebrates Emirati hospitality and culture. Curated by Tashkeel, we blend traditional flavours with modern technique in an elegant, welcoming setting.

The name speaks to the warmth and generosity at the heart of Arab culture — a table where every guest is family.
