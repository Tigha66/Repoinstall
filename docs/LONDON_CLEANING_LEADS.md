## ⏱️ 10-second qualification check

Message only if **3+ are true**:

- [ ] Contact is a single mobile (07…) or one local landline — not 0800 / online-only
- [ ] Personal or owner branding (a named person / small team)
- [ ] No live online booking widget / no staffed live chat
- [ ] Reviews mention "didn't pick up", "called me back", "hard to reach"
- [ ] Roughly < 50 reviews / clearly local, not multi-city
- [ ] Not VC-backed or franchise-with-call-centre

🚩 Mobile number + no booking system = almost certainly a solo operator losing jobs to voicemail. **Best target.**

## 💬 First-message templates (WhatsApp / SMS)

Keep it short, specific, and about *them* — not a pitch wall. Pick one.

### Template A — the direct "missed call" hook
> Hi [Name], I came across [Business] on Google — looks like you do great work around [Area]. Quick question: do you ever lose jobs because you're on-site and can't get to the phone? I help small cleaning firms catch those missed calls automatically. Happy to show you how it works in 2 mins if useful. No pressure either way 👍

### Template B — soft, curiosity-led
> Hi [Name], are you the right person to speak to about bookings at [Business]? I had a quick idea that might help you stop missing calls when you're out cleaning. Worth a 2-minute message?

### Template C — proof-first
> Hi [Name], saw a couple of reviews mention it's hard to reach [Business] by phone sometimes — totally normal when you're hands-on with clients. I set up a simple system that texts callers back instantly so you don't lose the job. Can I send a 30-sec example?

**Personalisation checklist before sending:**
- [ ] Real owner name (not "there")
- [ ] Correct, verified number
- [ ] Mention their actual area or a real review detail
- [ ] One clear, low-friction ask

## 📋 Compliance note (UK B2B outreach)

- Texting a business number for B2B is generally allowed, but **honour opt-outs immediately** and don't re-message people who say no.
- Identify yourself / your business clearly.
- Don't mass-blast — keep it personal and low volume.
- Verify each number from the business's own listing before sending.
