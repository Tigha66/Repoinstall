Important: the **product** delivers via SMS, but you **reach prospects** via **email + cold call +
LinkedIn** (don't cold-SMS US businesses — that has its own compliance issues). Pricing: **$99 /

## The pitch (what the product does)
"When a customer calls and you can't pick up, they instantly get a **text** ('Sorry we missed your
call — how can we help?') so the job doesn't go to the next contractor. Done-for-you, live in a day."

## Where to find US trades leads
- **Google Maps:** "HVAC [city]", "emergency plumber [city]", "electrician near [zip]".
- **Directories:** Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, BBB, Nextdoor business listings.
- **Scale:** scrape with Outscraper/Apify (name, site, phone, email) → verify emails → load into your
  cold-email tool (see `EMAIL-SCALE-SETUP.md`). Target owner-operated / small shops (1–15 trucks).
- Qualify: real reviews mentioning "didn't answer / hard to reach / went to voicemail" = perfect.

## Best US cities to target (high contractor density + spend)
Phoenix, Houston, Dallas, Atlanta, Tampa/Orlando, Las Vegas, Charlotte, Denver — big HVAC/plumbing
markets with hot weather (emergency call volume) and lots of small operators.
