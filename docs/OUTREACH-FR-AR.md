## 🏆 Top niches for Arabic & French markets
These markets are extremely **WhatsApp-first** and phone/booking-driven → even better fit than UK/US.

1. **Beauty — salons, barbers, spas/hammams** (صالون تجميل، حلاق / coiffeur, institut de beauté) ⭐
   Bookings by phone/WhatsApp, hands busy with clients = tons of missed calls. Huge volume everywhere.
2. **Private clinics & dentists** (عيادة أسنان، طب تجميلي / cabinet dentaire, médecine esthétique)
   High value per patient; aesthetic/dental clinics are booming in Morocco, Tunisia, Gulf.
3. **Restaurants / cafés / traiteurs (catering)** — reservations + WhatsApp orders/delivery.
4. **Instagram/WhatsApp boutiques & COD e-commerce** — these LIVE on WhatsApp; a missed message =
   a lost sale. Massive in Morocco, Algeria, Egypt, Gulf.
5. **Real estate agents** (سمسار / agent immobilier) — very phone/WhatsApp driven; one deal = big.
6. **Trades & home services** — plombier/électricien (plumber/electrician), AC repair (very big in Gulf).
7. **Auto — garages, car wash, car rental** (location de voiture — big in Morocco/Tunisia tourism).
8. **Driving schools & tutoring / gyms & coaches** (auto-école, coach sportif).

**Where to start:** Beauty (#1) + clinics (#2) are the easiest, highest-volume, best-paying entry
points in both FR and AR markets.

## ⚠️ Practical notes for these markets
- **WhatsApp is THE channel** → outreach via WhatsApp/Instagram DM often beats email (many SMBs
  barely use email). For email, target clinics/agencies/restaurants that publish one.
- **Telephony:** missed-call *detection* needs a local number — easy in France (Telnyx); in
  Morocco/Algeria use call-forwarding to an available number or a local provider. The WhatsApp
  text-back itself works from any OpenWA number, anywhere.
- **Pricing:** France ≈ €49/€99/€199. Maghreb lower (e.g. 490/990/1990 MAD; adjust for DZD/TND/Gulf).
- **Dialect:** Arabic templates are MSA (universal). For Morocco/Algeria you can switch to Darija +
  French mix later — but MSA converts fine to start.
