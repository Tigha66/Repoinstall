# 🌍 Top niches per city (Dubai · Paris · Canada · Casablanca)

Same product, but language, channel & price differ by city. Best universal entry niche everywhere:
**beauty salons** + **clinics** (high volume, phone/WhatsApp bookings, miss lots of calls).

## 🇦🇪 Dubai (UAE)
- **Language:** English (primary) + Arabic · **Channel:** WhatsApp / Instagram DM · **Price:** premium **$99/$199/$399**
- **Top niches:** 1) Beauty salons/barbers/nails/lashes/spas ⭐ 2) Aesthetic & dental clinics (high-value)
  3) Real estate brokers (huge, high-commission, phone-driven) 4) AC repair & home maintenance
  5) Restaurants/cloud kitchens (delivery) 6) Luxury car rental/detailing/garages
- Note: very WhatsApp-first + high willingness to pay → best premium market.

## 🇫🇷 Paris (France)
- **Language:** French · **Channel:** phone/email + Instagram DM · **Price:** **€49/€99/€199**
- **Top niches:** 1) Salons de coiffure / instituts de beauté / barbiers ⭐ 2) Cabinets dentaires /
  médecine esthétique / kinés-ostéos 3) Restaurants / cafés / traiteurs 4) Artisans & dépannage
  (plombier, électricien, serrurier) 5) Garages / contrôle technique 6) Agences immobilières
- Competitor to beat: *permanence téléphonique* (€39–€300/mo) — you undercut + automate.

## 🇨🇦 Canada (Montréal / Toronto)
- **Language:** French (Québec) / English (rest) · **Channel:** ⚠️ **SMS-leaning** (Canadians use
  SMS/iMessage more than WhatsApp) → lead with SMS for most; WhatsApp for immigrant communities.
  · **Price:** **C$69 / C$149 / C$299**
- **Top niches:** 1) Salons / barbershops / spas / esthetics ⭐ 2) Dental / physio / chiro clinics
  3) Trades — plumbers/electricians/**HVAC & furnace** (cold = urgent, high value) 4) Restaurants
  5) Auto — garages/detailing 6) Home services (snow removal, landscaping), real estate
- Note: Canada needs SMS A2P setup (like US) for SMS; WhatsApp works where the audience uses it.

## 🇲🇦 Casablanca (Morocco)
- **Language:** French + Arabic (Darija) · **Channel:** WhatsApp / Instagram DM (#1) · **Price:**
  lower — **~290 / 590 / 990 MAD**
- **Top niches:** 1) Salons de beauté / coiffure / spas-hammams ⭐ 2) Cliniques dentaires &
  esthétiques (medical tourism, booming) 3) Restaurants / cafés / traiteurs 4) **Instagram/WhatsApp
  boutiques + COD e-commerce** (they live on WhatsApp — missed msg = lost sale) 5) Location de
  voiture / garages / car wash 6) Auto-écoles, salles de sport, immobilier
- Note: WhatsApp is THE channel; first-mover advantage (little direct competition).

## 🚀 Where to start
**Beauty salons + clinics first** in every city — easiest to find (Instagram/Fresha/Google Maps),
highest call volume, clear ROI. Dubai = best premium money; Casablanca = easiest (your home turf,
WhatsApp-native); Paris/Canada = solid recurring with FR.
