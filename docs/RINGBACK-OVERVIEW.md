# 📣 RingBack — Project Overview, Promotion & Competitor Comparison

## 1. What RingBack is (one sentence)
**RingBack is a done-for-you "WhatsApp/SMS receptionist" that instantly texts back anyone whose
call a local business misses — so they stop losing customers to voicemail.**

When a customer calls a business and no one picks up (busy, on a job, after hours), RingBack
automatically sends that caller a friendly, branded message — *"Sorry we missed your call! How can
we help? Reply here."* — turning a lost call into a live conversation and a booked job.

## 2. How it works (simple)
```
Customer calls the business → not answered → Telnyx detects the missed call
   → RingBack instantly sends the caller a branded WhatsApp (UK) or SMS (US)
   → the customer replies → the business wins the job they'd otherwise have lost
```
- No new number, no app, no software for their team. Live in 24 hours, done-for-you.
- Add-ons: appointment reminders (cut no-shows), review requests, SMS fallback.

## 3. Why businesses pay (the ROI)
- Local businesses miss ~25–30% of calls at busy times; **most missed callers never call back —
  they ring a competitor.**
- Recovering **one** job a month usually pays the fee many times over (a plumber job = £150–£2k; a
  patient = £100s). That's why it's sticky recurring revenue.

## 4. Pricing
- **UK:** Starter £49/mo · Pro £99/mo · Managed £249/mo · + £99 setup (waived for founders).
- **US:** $99 / $199 / $399/mo (the US market pays more for the same thing).
- 7-day free trial, no card.

## 5. How to promote RingBack
**A. Direct outreach (fastest to first customers) — your main engine:**
- Niches: plumbers/heating, electricians, dentists, salons, garages (see `NICHES.md`).
- Channels: **call them** (if they don't answer, you just proved the problem — text the demo);
  WhatsApp their mobile; their website contact form; email; Instagram DM (salons).
- The hook: a 30-sec demo (get.callpilotvoice.co.uk) + free 7-day trial. Messages in
  `OUTREACH-TRADES-BEAUTY.md`, `LONDON_CLEANING_LEADS.md`, `EMAILS-TRADES-BEAUTY-BATCH.md`.
- The "build-trust" move: do the setup for them, show recovered calls, convert the trial.

**B. Demo-led:** the landing page + demo video (get.callpilotvoice.co.uk) is your proof — link it
in every message; record a 30-sec "call → text-back lands" clip.

**C. Referrals & testimonials:** first 3 founders → testimonial + 1 referral each → social proof
sells the next. Niche down ("we do this for 6 London plumbers") = warm intros.

**D. Local presence (slower, compounding):** Google Business listing, a few local-SEO posts,
LinkedIn posts showing recovered-call results, partnerships (web designers, business coaches).

**E. Paid (later, once unit economics proven):** Google/Facebook ads to the landing page targeting
"never miss a call" pains; cold email at scale via a dedicated domain (`EMAIL-SCALE-SETUP.md`).

## 6. Competitor comparison
| Service | What it does | Price/mo | How RingBack compares |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Podium** (US leader) | Missed-call text-back + reviews + payments | **$399–$599+** | RingBack = the core missed-call piece at a fraction of the price; Podium is heavy/expensive |
| **GoHighLevel** | All-in-one CRM/funnels (you *rent* the platform) | **$97–$497** (+ your time) | You *own* RingBack → ~95% margin; GHL is broader but you pay them + build it |
| **AI receptionists** (heyjodie, Norango, Ringly, Rosie) | Live AI *answers* calls | **£49–£350 / $50–$300** | RingBack is simpler/cheaper to deliver; can upsell AI answering later |
| **Human answering services** (Moneypenny, AllDayPA, alldayPA) | Live human receptionist | **£50–£300** (+ per-call) | RingBack is automated, 24/7, no per-call cost; cheaper to run |
| **Part-time receptionist** | A person | **£1,200–£1,800** | RingBack does the "never miss a call" job for £49 |
| **Twilio/360dialog (raw APIs)** | DIY building blocks | usage-based | RingBack is done-for-you; clients don't want to build |

**Your positioning in one line:**
> *"Podium charges $400+/month. A receptionist costs £1,200. RingBack does the part that actually
> wins you customers — texting back every missed call — for £49/month, done for you."*

## 7. Honest pros/cons to know
- **Pros:** cheap to run, recurring, clear ROI, huge addressable market, you own it.
- **Cons / watch-outs:** the UK WhatsApp engine is unofficial (ban risk at heavy volume → migrate
  clients to official WhatsApp Cloud API / SMS as you scale); US needs toll-free + verification;
  per-client a dedicated/lighter number is more stable.

## 8. The bottleneck (be honest)
Everything is built. The only thing between RingBack and revenue is **outreach volume** — calls/
messages to real businesses every day. Promotion = consistent direct outreach to the niches above.
