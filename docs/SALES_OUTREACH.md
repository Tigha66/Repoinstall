## Objection Handling

### "We already reply to all our messages"
> That's great! The AI isn't about replacing you — it's about the messages you miss. Even the best teams can't reply instantly at 11pm or when they're on a job. The AI captures those overnight/weekend enquiries so you never lose them. Would it help to see what that looks like?

### "I don't trust AI to talk to my customers"
> Totally fair. The AI is designed to be transparent — customers know they're talking to an assistant, and it always offers to connect them with a human. Plus, you can test it for 2 weeks before going live with real customers. Most clients find customers actually prefer it because they get an instant response.

### "How much does it cost?"
> It depends on the package, but most cleaning companies I work with pay £X/month. Considering that one missed lead can be worth £X-£X in cleaning fees, it usually pays for itself within the first week. I can give you exact pricing after I understand your volume.

### "We don't get many WhatsApp enquiries"
> No problem — when you do get them, are they usually from people ready to book? Those are the most valuable leads, and they often come at inconvenient times (evenings, weekends). The AI makes sure none of those slip through. Even capturing 2-3 extra leads a month would cover the cost.

### "I need to think about it"
> Of course! Here's what I'd suggest: let me set up a free pilot with your WhatsApp number. You test it for 2 weeks with your team — no customers see it yet. At the end, you'll have real data on how many extra leads it captures. If it doesn't work for you, no charge. Fair?

## Prospecting Sources (London Cleaning Companies)

> 💡 A pre-researched, qualified shortlist already exists: [`LONDON_CLEANING_LEADS.md`](./LONDON_CLEANING_LEADS.md). Use it first, then top up with the sources below.

### Where to Find Prospects
1. **Google Maps** — Search "cleaning company London", check WhatsApp numbers in listings
2. **Yell.com** — Business directory, filter by cleaning services
3. **Bark.com** — Service marketplace, cleaning category
4. **Gumtree** — Cleaning service ads (these businesses are actively marketing)
5. **Facebook Groups** — Local community groups, cleaning service recommendations
6. **Instagram** — Search #LondonCleaning #CleaningCompanyLondon
7. **Nextdoor** — Local recommendations
8. **Referrals** — Ask your wife's network, friends, family

### What to Look For
- ✅ They list a WhatsApp number on their website/profile (shows they use WhatsApp for business)
- ✅ They serve a local area (not national franchises)
- ✅ They offer multiple services (regular, deep, end-of-tenancy)
- ✅ They have Google reviews but might have slow response times
- ❌ National chains / franchises (too complex for pilot)
- ❌ No WhatsApp presence (not ready for this yet)

## What NOT to Do

1. ❌ **NEVER** send bulk WhatsApp messages — instant ban
2. ❌ **NEVER** cold message from the client's WhatsApp number
3. ❌ **NEVER** spam Facebook/Instagram comment sections
4. ❌ **NEVER** buy contact lists
5. ❌ **NEVER** pretend to be a customer to get a reply
6. ❌ **NEVER** go live without client approval and testing
7. ✅ **DO** get referrals from happy clients
8. ✅ **DO** collect case studies and testimonials
9. ✅ **DO** ask for Google reviews from satisfied clients
10. ✅ **DO** build relationships — one happy client refers 3 more
