## 🍽️ How RingBack works for Restaurants / Cafés / Traiteurs
Restaurants miss a LOT of calls during service — and each missed call is **direct lost revenue**
(a reservation, a takeaway order, or a catering job). RingBack turns each into a WhatsApp.

**The use cases:**
1. **Missed reservation call** → instant WhatsApp with a **reserve link** (or "reply with date/time/
   party size"). → table booked instead of lost.
2. **Missed takeaway / delivery order** (the big one during rush) → instant WhatsApp: "We were
   slammed! Order here 👉 [menu/order link] or reply with your order." → recover the order =
   immediate money.
3. **Traiteur / catering enquiry** (high value) → missed call → WhatsApp captures the lead: "Send us
   the date, number of guests & event type and we'll send a quote." → big job saved.
4. **After-hours calls** → WhatsApp with opening hours + reserve/order link.

**The `{booking}` link for a restaurant can be:** a reservation tool (Cal.com / their booking
system), their **online menu/order page** (own site, WhatsApp catalog, Glovo/Jahez link), or simply
"reply here and we'll take it" (staff handle it in WhatsApp, which they already watch).

### Restaurant text-back templates
**🇫🇷 French**
> Bonjour 👋 Désolé d'avoir manqué votre appel chez {business} ! Réservez une table ou commandez
> ici : {booking} — ou répondez à ce message (date, heure, nombre de personnes, ou votre commande)
> et on s'occupe de tout. ✨

**🇲🇦 Arabic**
> مرحباً 👋 نعتذر عن عدم الرد على مكالمتك في {business}! احجز طاولة أو اطلب من هنا: {booking} — أو
> راسلنا (التاريخ، الوقت، عدد الأشخاص، أو طلبك) وسنتكفّل بالباقي. ✨

**🇬🇧 English**
> Hi 👋 Sorry we missed your call to {business}! Reserve a table or order here: {booking} — or just
> reply (date, time, party size, or your order) and we'll sort it. ✨

**Why restaurants buy it:** one recovered takeaway order a day already pays the monthly fee; a saved
catering booking pays for a year. Pair with **reminders** (reduce no-show reservations) for the Pro tier.
