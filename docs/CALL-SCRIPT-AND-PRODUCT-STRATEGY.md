# ☎️ Trades Call Script + 🧭 Product Strategy (voice agents & chatbots)

## PART 1 — Phone-call script for the trades list
Call the **mobile (07…)** numbers first. Keep it human and short. Two paths:

### If they ANSWER (15-sec opener)
> "Hi, is this [name/the owner]? I'll be quick — I'm Abdelhak, I run a little service for plumbers
> and trades. When you're on a job and can't grab the phone, that customer just rings the next
> plumber, right? I set up a system that instantly texts those missed callers back so you don't
> lose the work. I'm setting it up **free for 7 days** for a few [area] trades this week — can I
> send you a 30-second demo on WhatsApp?"

- If yes → get their WhatsApp number, send the demo link (get.callpilotvoice.co.uk).
- "How much?" → "Free for 7 days; £49/mo after only if you keep it — let's first see what it catches."
- "I don't miss many calls" → "Perfect, then it's cheap insurance and catches the few you do —
  those emergency ones are the big jobs. Free to try?"
- "Send me info" → get WhatsApp/email, send demo + follow up in 2 days.

### If it goes to VOICEMAIL (this is GOLD — you just proved the problem)
Leave this voicemail, then text the same:
> "Hi, I just tried calling and got voicemail — which is actually exactly what I help with! When a
> customer can't reach you, they ring the next trade. I set you up so every missed call gets an
> instant text back. 30-sec demo: get.callpilotvoice.co.uk — free for 7 days. It's Abdelhak — text
> me back if you'd like it on."
**Text (WhatsApp) right after:** "Hi — Abdelhak here, just left you a voicemail 🙂 I help trades
never miss a call: missed call → instant text-back. 30-sec demo: get.callpilotvoice.co.uk. Free
7-day trial, I set it all up. Worth a look?"

**Cadence:** 10 calls/day. Voicemail → text. No reply → 1 follow-up text in 2 days, then stop.

## PART 2 — Should you sell AI Voice Agents & website Chatbots? (honest)

### 🎙️ AI Voice Agents (AI answers the phone, books, answers FAQs)
**Verdict: great PREMIUM UPSELL — not your first product.**
- ✅ High value + price ($199–$499/mo), hot market, strong ROI story, recurring.
- ❌ Hard to build/maintain (latency, accuracy, hallucinations); a **bad AI call = lost customer +
  angry client = churn + reputation risk**; per-minute voice costs; heavy per-client tuning;
  crowded (VAPI/Retell/Synthflow agencies everywhere).
- **Best use:** the natural Tier-2 upgrade from RingBack. Land a client on missed-call text-back
  (£49), prove value, THEN upsell "want the AI to actually answer & book the calls? £249/mo."
  Sell it once you have happy clients + the skills — don't lead with it.

### 💬 Website Chatbots (embed a chat widget on client sites)
**Verdict: weak as a standalone paid product for local SMBs — use as a free add-on at best.**
- ✅ Easy to build/embed (one script tag), low risk, cheap to run.
- ❌ **Commoditised** (Tidio/Crisp/Intercom/free options); **low perceived value & low willingness
  to pay**; local trades/salons get little web traffic, so the bot has nothing to do → high churn,
  weak MRR, fuzzy ROI.
- **Best use:** throw it in **free** with a website build as a "lead capture" sweetener — not a
  product you charge meaningful MRR for. (Exception: high-traffic e-commerce/B2B — different game.)

### 🧭 The strategy (important)
You don't need new products — **you have the right one.** Ranked for *you, now*:
1. **RingBack missed-call text-back** — simplest to sell + deliver, real ROI, low risk. **Your beachhead.**
2. **AI voice agent** — premium **upsell** to RingBack clients later (£199–£399/mo). Higher reward, higher effort/risk.
3. **Website + chatbot** — website = one-off cash hook; chatbot = free sweetener, not paid MRR.

**UK vs US:** both markets want all three. US pays ~2× (voice agents $300–$500 common) and uses
SMS not WhatsApp. UK is your home turf and live now. **Win UK with RingBack first, then layer voice
agents as the upsell, then expand to US.**

> The trap to avoid: chasing voice agents/chatbots as NEW builds = more half-finished repos, zero
> sales. Sell RingBack first. Add voice only when a paying client asks for "more."
