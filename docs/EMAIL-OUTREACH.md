## Sending rules (stay safe + compliant)
- **~15–20/day max** from a normal inbox. No bulk blasts.
- **Verify the email** on their Contact page first (bounces wreck deliverability).
- **Personalise the opening line** (already done above) — it's what gets replies.
- **Honour opt-outs** immediately; never re-email a "no".
- Identify yourself (the signature does this) — that's the UK B2B requirement.
- Log each send in `trial-tracker.csv`; follow up twice then stop.
