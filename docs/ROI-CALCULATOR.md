# ROI — why the price is a no-brainer

Use this in the trial→paid conversation. Plug in *their* numbers.

## The simple version
```
Missed calls per month            =  M
% of missed callers you recover    =  R   (text-back typically 10–25%)
Average value of one customer      =  £V

Extra revenue / month  =  M × R × £V
```

## Worked examples

**Barber** (avg cut £20, ~3 visits/yr ⇒ ~£60/yr value)
- Misses ~40 calls/mo, recovers 15% ⇒ 6 customers
- 6 × £60 = **£360/mo recovered** vs **£49/mo** → ~7× ROI

**Dental / aesthetics clinic** (avg treatment £150+)
- Misses ~60 calls/mo, recovers 12% ⇒ ~7 patients
- 7 × £150 = **£1,050/mo recovered** vs **£99/mo** → ~10× ROI

**Plumber / trades** (avg job £120)
- Misses ~30 calls/mo, recovers 20% ⇒ 6 jobs
- 6 × £120 = **£720/mo recovered** vs **£49/mo** → ~14× ROI

## The one-liner for the close
> "You only need to recover **one** customer a month for this to pay for itself.
> During the trial it recovered **[X]**. The maths makes itself."

## Honest framing (builds trust, closes better)
Don't over-claim recovery %. Say: *"Even if it catches just a handful of the calls
you'd otherwise lose, it more than covers itself — and you saw it work on real
callers during the trial."* Let the trial's actual numbers do the talking.
