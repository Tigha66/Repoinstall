# 🎯 Best Niches for Missed-Call Text-Back (UK & US)

What makes a great target: **high-value jobs** (so one recovered call pays the fee many times),
**owner-operated / phone-driven**, **often away from the phone** (on a job/with a client), and
**urgent enquiries** (caller will just ring the next business if you don't answer).

> ⚠️ **UK vs US channel:** In the **UK/Europe** lead with **WhatsApp** (what's built). In the
> **US**, people use **SMS**, not WhatsApp — lead with **SMS text-back** (your service already
> has Telnyx SMS; make it primary for US clients). Same product, different channel.

## 🇬🇧 UK — top niches (start order)
1. **Trades / home services** ⭐ — plumbers, electricians, gas/heating, locksmiths, roofers,
   pest control, garage-door, landscapers. Always on a job, urgent calls, £100–£2,000 jobs. Best ROI.
2. **Beauty & personal care** — barbers, hair/nail salons, spas, lash/brow, tattoo studios.
   Hands busy with clients = constant missed calls; bookings-driven.
3. **Health & clinics** — dental, aesthetics/med-spa, physio, chiropractor, private GP, opticians,
   vets. High treatment value (£150+), no-show sensitive (upsell reminders).
4. **Auto** — independent garages/MOT, body shops, mobile mechanics, tyre/car detailing. Under a
   car = can't answer; quote-driven.
5. **Cleaning** (your current niche) + **removals / man-and-van** — quote & deadline driven.
6. **Property** — small estate/letting agents, property management.

## 🇺🇸 US — top niches (lead with SMS)
*(These are exactly Podium's market — proven to pay $200–$400/mo, so you can charge more.)*
1. **HVAC / plumbing / electrical** ⭐ — huge, urgent, high-ticket; the #1 missed-call-text-back market.
2. **Roofing & home remodeling / contractors** — big job values, lead-gen heavy.
3. **Dental & med-spa / aesthetics** — high revenue per patient, reminder upsell.
4. **Auto repair & dealerships (service dept)** — constant inbound, easy to miss.
5. **Law firms (small — PI, family, immigration)** — one client = thousands; they pay well.
6. **Real estate agents & property management** — time-sensitive buyer/tenant calls.
7. **Restaurants / salons / fitness studios** — bookings & reservations.

## How to pitch each (one line to swap in)
- Trades: "When you're under a sink/on a roof, that call just rings the next guy — we text them back instantly."
- Beauty: "Hands busy with a client? The caller books elsewhere. We catch them on WhatsApp/SMS automatically."
- Dental/clinics: "A missed new-patient call is £150+ gone — and we cut no-shows with reminders too."
- Auto: "Head under a bonnet = missed booking. We auto-text every missed caller back."
- Law (US): "One missed call can be a $5k case walking to a competitor — we text them back in seconds."

## Pricing by market
- **UK:** £49 / £99 / £249 (as set).
- **US:** charge **$99 / $199 / $399** — the market pays it (Podium charges $400+ for similar).

## Expansion strategy
Nail **one niche** (get 3–5 paying + testimonials), clone the landing/message for the next,
then repeat. Niche testimonials sell the next client in that niche ("we do this for 6 London
plumbers"). Don't spread thin early — depth in one niche beats one-of-everything.
