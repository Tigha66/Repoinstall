Combines your two best assets: **525 ready website templates** (`beautiful-websites`) +
**RingBack missed-call text-back** (live). One offer, higher value, easier close than AI alone.

## The offer
> **A professional website for your business + a system that instantly texts back every missed
> call — so you get found online AND stop losing the customers who ring you.**

**Pricing (UK):**
- **£399 one-off** (website built from a template, branded to them) **+ £49/mo** (hosting +

Why it closes: a website is tangible and instantly understood; the missed-call piece adds ongoing

## ICP — who to target
Local businesses that **have no website (or an old/ugly one) AND miss calls**: trades
(plumbers/electricians/roofers), cleaners, salons/barbers, garages, landscapers. You already have
lead sources: `no-website-finder`, `lead-generation-results` (420+ prospects), Google Maps.

## 🔑 The killer tactic: send a FREE sample homepage
This converts web design 5–10× better than a plain pitch:
1. Pick a prospect. Grab their name, logo/photos, services from their Google listing / Facebook.
2. Take the best-fitting template from your 525, swap in their name + a hero line + their services
   (~15–20 min). Deploy it to a temp URL (Netlify/Vercel/GitHub Pages — free).
3. Send: *"I built you a homepage — here's the live preview: [link]. Want it as your real site? I'll
   also set you up so you never miss a call."*
Seeing their own business already online is what makes them say yes.

## 📅 2-week sell plan (NO building of new products — only selling)
**Day 1:** Pick your 1 best template to demo. Write the message above. List **20 local businesses
with no/weak website** (Google Maps + `no-website-finder`).
**Days 2–10:** Each day — build **3 free sample homepages** for 3 prospects (template + their
details) → send the message with the preview link. ~3/day = momentum without burnout.
**Day 3 onward:** Follow up non-repliers once (+2 days).
**On a "yes":** take their content → finish the site (template) → point their domain → set up
RingBack (`onboard.sh`) → invoice **£249 + £39/mo** (founder deal).
**Day 14:** Target — ~25 previews sent → 3–5 interested → 1–2 paying (£249 + £39/mo each).

- Sample-site preview link in every message — that's the hook.
