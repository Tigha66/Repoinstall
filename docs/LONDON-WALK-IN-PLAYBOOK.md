# 🚶 London walk-in sales playbook — RingBack (door-to-door)

Face-to-face = fastest path to your first paying customer. No ban risk, no spam filter, live demo on
their phone. Goal each outing: 20–30 conversations → 3–5 live demos → 1–2 free trials started.

## Best niches (start with the top 3)
1. Barbershops ⭐  2. Hair/beauty salons, nail bars, lash/brow ⭐  3. Cafés/restaurants/takeaways
4. Phone-repair shops  5. Garages/MOT  6. Tattoo studios  7. Dental/aesthetic clinics (Pro/Managed)
8. Dry cleaners, pet grooming, estate agents, PT studios

## Best London high streets
Shoreditch/Hackney Rd · Dalston/Kingsland · Walthamstow High St · Green Lanes (Harringay) ·
Peckham/Rye Lane · Brixton · Tooting · Whitechapel · Edgware Rd · Southall · Wembley · Ilford · East Ham · Croydon.
Pick ONE street, walk both sides, enter every target shop.

## When
Weekday 10:00–11:30 or 14:00–16:00 (the lulls). Avoid lunch rush, Saturdays, and packed shops (return later).

## What to carry
- Phone with the **demo ready and pre-tested that morning** (fire one to your own phone first).
- A few **business cards / one-page flyers** with a **QR code** to https://get.callpilotvoice.co.uk
- The booking demo open in a tab. Smart-casual dress. Smile. Notebook/phone sheet to log leads.

## ☎️ THE 30-SECOND PITCH (memorise it)
Walk in, friendly, find the owner/senior person:
> "Hi! Are you the owner? I'll be quick — I'm Abdelhak, I'm local. Quick question: when you're cutting/
> with a client and the phone rings and you can't grab it… that customer usually just calls the next
> place, right? I built a little tool that fixes that — can I show you on YOUR phone in 10 seconds?"

If yes → go straight to the live demo. Don't over-explain. Show, don't tell.

## 📲 THE LIVE DEMO (the magic moment)
1. "What's your mobile number? I'll pretend a customer just called you and couldn't get through."
2. On your phone, open this (replace with THEIR mobile, no + or spaces, keep country code 44):
   `https://get.callpilotvoice.co.uk/simulate?from=THEIR_MOBILE&to=442038389029&niche=barber&lang=en`
   (niche options: barber, salon, restaurant, clinic, cleaning, general · lang: en/fr/ar)
3. Their phone buzzes — they get an **instant WhatsApp** from "your business": "Sorry we missed your
   call — reply with a time and we'll book you in / book here: [link]."
4. "That's it. Every missed call gets that automatically — even after hours or on your day off. They
   reply, you book them. You keep the customer instead of losing them."
   ⚠️ Test the demo on your own phone each morning first (needs the WhatsApp service number live).
   Backup if WhatsApp is down: play the 30-sec demo video on https://get.callpilotvoice.co.uk

## 💷 Price (only when they ask)
"Plans from £49/month, and the first 7 days are free — I set it all up for you, nothing to install."
(Pro £99 = adds the booking link + reminders · Managed £199 = we run it + AI booking.)
Tip: test higher — £79/£149/£299 — you're far under Podium ($399+) / Weave ($249).

## 🛡️ Objection handling
- "I'm too busy right now" → "No worries — 10 seconds, watch your phone." (or) "When's quieter, I'll pop back?"
- "I already answer my WhatsApp/phone" → "Love it — this is for the calls you CAN'T grab: after hours,
  day off, or mid-haircut. It only fires when you miss one. Want to see?"
- "How much?" → give price + "first 7 days free, I set it up."
- "Send me info" → "Sure — but it lands better in 10 seconds now. Can I just show you?" Then if still no,
  hand the QR flyer + take their WhatsApp to follow up.
- "I don't really use WhatsApp" → "No problem, it can text by SMS instead." (Pro plan)
- "Does it cost me per message / new number?" → "No new number, no per-message fee. Uses your existing line."

## ✅ THE CLOSE (start the free trial on the spot)
> "Let's just switch on your free 7-day trial now — no card. I'll need your business name and the
> WhatsApp number you want it on, and it's live within a day. If it wins you customers, you keep it
> for £49/mo. If not, no worries. Deal?"
Get: **business name + WhatsApp number + best contact**. Tell them you'll confirm it's live by tomorrow.
→ Send Abdelhak the name + number → onboard via onboard.sh.

## Soft close (if not ready)
Hand the QR flyer, take their WhatsApp, agree to send the demo link. Log them for follow-up in 2–3 days.

## Daily targets & tracking
- Target: 20–30 doors, 3–5 demos, 1–2 trials per outing.
- Log each: business, niche, person, outcome (demo'd / trial / follow-up / no), WhatsApp number.
- It's a numbers game — most say no; you only need a few yeses to build MRR.

## Mindset
You're not begging — you're showing local owners how to stop losing money. Be quick, warm, confident.
Walk away politely if they're slammed. Every "no" gets you closer to a "yes". Aim for reps, not perfection.
