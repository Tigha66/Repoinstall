## 2. The MRR maths (your targets)
Average revenue per customer ≈ **£80–100/mo**. So:

| Customers | ~MRR | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | ~£450 | Proof — your first founders |
| 12 | ~£1,000 | Side income, validated |
| 35 | ~£3,000 | Replaces a salary |
| 60 | ~£5,500 | Real business |

**Funnel reality:** ~20 contacts → ~4 trials → ~2 paying. So **~10 quality contacts per paying customer.** To get 5 customers, plan ~50 contacts. That's one focused week.

## 3. Your unfair advantage: start with the cleaning niche
You run a cleaning business, so you *understand* it and have peers. **Niche down first** — sell only to **cleaning companies** (then expand). Benefits:
- You speak their language; your own business is the live proof.
- One message/landing tweak fits all of them.
- Referrals compound inside a tight niche.

Your pitch becomes: *"I built this for my own cleaning company — it caught [X] missed jobs last week. I'll set it up free for yours for 7 days."*

## 4. The 14-day launch sprint
**Days 1–2 — Make the proof undeniable**
- Finish linking the demo WhatsApp; do a real call test.
- Record a **60–90s Loom**: call the number → show the WhatsApp land. (Loom.com, free.)
- Add the Loom link + your WhatsApp button to the landing page (ask me, I'll wire it).

**Days 3–4 — Build the list (target: 40–50 cleaning businesses)**
- Google Maps: "cleaning company London", "end of tenancy cleaning [area]", "office cleaning [area]".
- Also: Bark, Checkatrade, Yell, Instagram (#londoncleaning), local FB groups.
- Log them in `lead-list-template.csv` (Name, area, phone, website, channel).

**Days 5–9 — Outreach (use `outreach-scripts.md`)**
- 10 contacts/day across: their website WhatsApp button, Instagram DM, email (hello@callpilotvoice.co.uk), and a few calls.
- Lead with the **Loom + free trial**, never price.
- Follow up twice (most replies come on follow-up #1).

**Days 8–14 — Run trials & convert**
- Set each trial up for them (you connect their WhatsApp, brand the message).
- Track recovered calls in `trial-tracker.csv`.
- End of trial: show the number ("it texted back X callers, Y replied") → ask for the card.
- **Founder deal:** first 3 lock in £49/mo for life + free setup, for a testimonial + 1 referral.

## 6. Conversion mechanics (what turns trials into MRR)
- **Do the setup for them** = sunk-cost + zero effort to say yes.
- **Show recovered money**, not features. One recovered £150 end-of-tenancy job = 3 months of Starter.
- **Monthly, cancel-anytime** removes risk; they stay because it works.
- **Ask for the testimonial** the moment they say something positive → put it on the landing page → it sells the next one.

## 7. Retention & growth (keep the MRR)
- Send a simple **monthly "we recovered X callers for you" message** — reminds them of value, kills churn.
- **Referrals:** "know another [cleaning] business? Free month for both if they sign up."
- Expand niches once cleaning works: barbers, salons, dentists, trades, driving instructors.

## 8. Honest risks to plan around
- **Stability:** use a dedicated/light WhatsApp number per client; the watchdog auto-heals.
- **WhatsApp bans:** the unofficial engine can get numbers banned under heavy use. Fine for
  trials/early clients — but **migrate to the official WhatsApp Cloud API** before scaling past
  ~10–15 clients. Build that into your pricing (Cloud API has per-message fees).
- **Compliance (UK):** you only message people who **just called the client** = expected,
  service-level contact. Keep it to that; don't send marketing blasts.
