## ⚔️ Competitors & what they charge (by market)
**🇫🇷 France** — the thing you replace:
- **Permanence téléphonique externalisée** (human call answering): **€39–€300/mo** + €1–2.50/call
  (e.g. Permanance, Contaxium, Efficall). · AI receptionists: ~€30–€150 self-serve, €99–€350 managed.
- **RingBack edge:** automated, 24/7, no per-call fee, WhatsApp-native, done-for-you — at €49 you
  sit *under* the human services while doing the "never miss a call" job they charge €150+ for.

**🇲🇦 Maghreb (Morocco/Algeria/Tunisia)** — little direct competition:
- Mostly **call-centre/télésecrétariat** services (variable, often cheaper labour) and **WhatsApp
  Business tools** (Wati-type, ~$20–80/mo, DIY). No dominant missed-call-text-back product.
- **RingBack edge:** first-mover, done-for-you, WhatsApp-native (the #1 channel there), local-language.

**🇦🇪🇸🇦 Gulf** — more SaaS spend, WhatsApp-first:
- **WhatsApp platforms** (Unifonic, Wati, Zoko): ~$30–$300/mo (DIY messaging, not missed-call
  text-back). Premium human answering services exist at higher prices.
- **RingBack edge:** specific missed-call recovery + done-for-you; Gulf pays readily ($99–$399).
