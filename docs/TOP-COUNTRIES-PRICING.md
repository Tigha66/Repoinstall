# 🌍 Top countries to sell RingBack (WhatsApp/OpenWA) + pricing competitiveness

## Selection logic
RingBack sends via WhatsApp (OpenWA) → the #1 filter is **WhatsApp penetration**.
Then: willingness-to-pay × founder languages (EN/FR/AR) × weak "done-for-you" competition.
⛔ US / Canada / Australia are SMS/iMessage markets → use the SMS variant there, NOT OpenWA WhatsApp.

## Competitor benchmarks (2025/26 research)
- Missed-call text-back software: Podium **$399+** (quote, up to ~$500), Weave **$249**,
  SalesCaptain $159, Chekkit $127–149, Textedly $29+.
- Human virtual receptionist: $79–$800/mo, or usage-based (Spain/Germany ~€0.27/min + fees).
- AI receptionist: from ~$29/mo (DIY) to €99–350 managed.
- WhatsApp DIY platforms (Wati/Zoko/ChatMaxima): $19–$300/mo + Meta per-message fees
  (Brazil marketing msg $0.0625, Mexico $0.0305).
- France permanence téléphonique: €39–€300/mo + per-call.

## Recommendation
Focus **Tier 1 (Gulf + UK)** — best pay, your languages, leads already gathered, and your price
beats Podium-class incumbents (you're arguably underpriced → test higher Pro/Managed).
Then **Spain / Italy / France** for EU volume. Use **Maghreb** as easy home-turf proving ground.
Avoid premium plays in Brazil/Mexico/India (cheap local WhatsApp tools); only enter with low local pricing.
