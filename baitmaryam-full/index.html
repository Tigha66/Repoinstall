Our Story

A table set with love

Named after Chef Salam Dakkak's mother, Maryam, this passionately run home brings the soul of the Levant to Dubai — recipes carried across generations and cooked exactly the way family should be fed.

A warm welcome, a quiet lakeside room, and food made with love.
