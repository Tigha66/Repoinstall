The Pub

From the marsh

Chef Stephen Harris turned a run-down pub on the Kent coast into one of Britain's most admired restaurants — self-taught, hyper-local, and Michelin-starred.

Salt made from the sea outside, oysters from the bay, lamb from the marsh. Still, simply, a pub.
