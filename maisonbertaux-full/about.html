A bohemian
Soho institution

Founded by French Communard refugees in 1871, Maison Bertaux is London's oldest patisserie — a tiny, much-loved café on Greek Street that has fed Soho's artists and dreamers for over 150 years.

Hand-made, every morning. Unchanged, and all the better for it.
