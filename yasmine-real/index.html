قصر الياسمين

A palace at The Pearl

On the waterfront of Porto Arabia, Yasmine Palace brings the grandeur of the Levantine and Qatari table to Doha — four distinct dining worlds, each with its own mood, beneath hand-carved arches and lantern light.

Generous mezze, charcoal grills and Gulf pots, served with the warmth and ceremony of true palace hospitality.
