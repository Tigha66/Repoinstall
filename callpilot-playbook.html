2 · How it actually works for the client (know this cold)

  1. They keep their existing number. We set their phone to forward unanswered/busy calls to their CallPilot number (every UK/US carrier supports "forward on no-answer").
  2. CallPilot answers 24/7, greets with their business name, has a natural conversation, answers FAQs, books the job / takes a message.
  3. After each call they get a text/email with the caller's name, number, address and reason.
  4. Setup takes a day. Nothing to install. They cancel anytime.

That's the whole product. You're selling "never miss a job again" for less than the cost of one lost job a month.
