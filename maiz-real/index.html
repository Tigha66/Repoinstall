ميز · Maiz

Born in Diriyah

Overlooking the UNESCO-listed At-Turaif in historic Diriyah, Maiz brings authentic Saudi cuisine into a thematic cultural setting — a journey through the thirteen regions of the Kingdom, with Najd at its heart.

Heritage grains, dried lime and slow fire, reimagined with modern technique and served with the warmth of a Saudi welcome.
