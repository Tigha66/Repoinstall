Our Story

St James's · Since 1742

1742

Nearly three
centuries of service

Wiltons began in 1742 as a stall selling oysters, shrimps and cockles, and grew into one of London's most distinguished restaurants. Today, on Jermyn Street in St James's, it remains a byword for the finest British oysters, fish and game.

Timeless, elegant, and impeccably served — a London institution across the centuries.
