## What is CallPilot?

CallPilot is an **AI phone receptionist** for small businesses — plumbers, electricians, HVAC, salons, clinics and other trades that lose work when calls go to voicemail.

When a business is busy on a job, the phone rings out and the caller dials the next company. CallPilot answers instead: 24/7, in the business's own name, in a natural human-sounding voice. It has a real conversation, answers FAQs, books the appointment, and instantly texts/emails the owner the caller's **name, number, address and reason for calling**.

> One job you don't miss pays for a whole year of CallPilot.

## Key features

- ☎️ **Answers every call 24/7** — after-hours, lunch, on-the-job, overflow
- 🗣️ **Natural conversation** — sounds like a real front-desk person
- 📅 **Books appointments** and captures lead details
- 📩 **Instant summaries** by text + email after every call
- 🔢 **Keeps the customer's existing number** (forward-on-no-answer)
- 🌍 **UK & US** — local demo lines in both markets

## How it works

1. The business **keeps its existing number** and forwards unanswered/busy calls to CallPilot.
2. CallPilot answers in the business's name, handles the conversation, and books the job.
3. The owner receives a text/email with the caller's details.
4. Setup takes a day. No hardware. Cancel anytime.
