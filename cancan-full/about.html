Carytown

A little piece
of Paris

Can Can Brasserie brought the authentic Parisian brasserie to Carytown — zinc bar, mosaic floors, classic banquettes, and a menu of French staples done right.

For over twenty years it has been a Richmond institution for oysters, steak frites, and an unforgettable weekend brunch.
