Una Tradición

Half a century of
interior Mexico

Since 1975, Fonda San Miguel has brought the regional cooking of interior Mexico to Austin — mole from Puebla, cochinita from the Yucatán, seafood from Veracruz — served beneath hand-carved beams and a celebrated collection of original Mexican art.

A landmark of hospitality, recognized across five decades and celebrated this year with our 50th Anniversary Cookbook.
