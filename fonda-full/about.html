Our Story

Austin · Since 1975

1975

A landmark of
interior Mexico

Founded in 1975, Fonda San Miguel introduced Austin to the authentic regional cooking of interior Mexico — long before it was celebrated elsewhere. Mole from Puebla, cochinita from the Yucatán, snapper from Veracruz.

The room itself is part of the experience: hand-carved beams, blooming courtyards, and one of the city's finest private collections of original Mexican art.
